<h1>🎠 Design Cart 3D Carousel</h1>
<h3>Interactive Three.js Image Carousel for WordPress</h3>
<p><strong>Turn ordinary image galleries into immersive, interactive 3D experiences.</strong></p>
<p>Design Cart 3D Carousel is a free, open-source WordPress plugin that transforms your photographs into rotating, multi-layered 3D rings using Three.js and WebGL.</p>
<p>Unlike traditional sliders, images are positioned in a real three-dimensional scene, allowing visitors to explore rotating photo rings, change perspectives, interact with animations, and experience atmospheric shader-based fog.</p>
<p>Designed for creative portfolios, photography websites, product showcases, architecture galleries, and projects that deserve something beyond an ordinary image slider.</p>

<h2>💡 Why I Built This Plugin</h2>
<p>The idea started with a client who asked me to create something truly unique for her website.</p>
<p>She didn't want another conventional image slider or a standard photo gallery.</p>
<p>She wanted something different. Something visitors would notice and remember.</p>
<p>Instead of adapting an existing carousel, I decided to experiment with a real 3D environment.</p>
<p>The concept was simple: what if photographs could be arranged around multiple rotating rings, suspended in a three-dimensional space?</p>
<p>What if visitors could interact with the camera, rotate the gallery, navigate between layers, and explore images surrounded by animated fog?</p>
<p>That experiment eventually became <strong>Design Cart 3D Carousel</strong>.</p>
<p>Initially created for a custom website project, I later transformed the solution into a configurable WordPress plugin that anyone can use.</p>

<h2>🚀 Installation</h2>
<ol start="1">
<li>
<p>Download the Design Cart 3D Carousel ZIP package.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Open your WordPress administration panel.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Navigate to <strong>Plugins → Add New Plugin → Upload Plugin</strong>.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Install and activate the plugin.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Open <strong>3D Carousel</strong> in the administration menu.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Create a new carousel.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Upload your images.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Customize the scene and animation.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Copy the generated shortcode to your page.</p>
</li>
</ol>
<h2>🔗 Shortcode Integration</h2>
<p>Embed your carousel in any WordPress content area that supports shortcodes.</p>
<pre><code>[dc_3d_carousel id="123"]</code></pre>
<p>Replace <code>123</code> with your carousel ID.</p>
<p>Alternative shortcode names are also supported:</p>
<pre><code>[dc-3d-carousel id="123"]
[design-cart-3d-carousel id="123"]</code></pre>
<p>The plugin supports multiple separately configured carousels.</p>

<h2>⚡ Performance Considerations</h2>
<p>Design Cart 3D Carousel uses real-time WebGL rendering.</p>
<p>For the best experience:</p>
<ul>
<li>
<p>Optimize and compress images before uploading.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Use appropriate image dimensions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Avoid unnecessarily large galleries.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Adjust the maximum pixel ratio for performance.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Test complex scenes on mobile devices.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Balance animation effects with usability.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Performance depends on the number of images, scene complexity, browser support, and the visitor's device.</p>
